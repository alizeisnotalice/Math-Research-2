# R72 shared whole-cell area optimization and exact uniqueization

Actual computation date: 2026-11-16, Asia/Shanghai. This component owns
two finite-area MIP experiments, their exact positive primal certificates,
one purposeful outside-mass contraction, and source-bound comparisons with
the separate complete-halo production. It does not own MATLAB or the
independent halo audit, and does not claim a new general bound for C2.

## Fixed input family and exact cell interface

S is the 5 by 5 uniform grid `(6i/25,6j/25)`, i,j=0,...,4,
with each mass 1/25. Candidate outside positions are the R71 144-point
grid (same step, indices -4 through 8, excluding S) plus the single
R71 oracle position `(-3/10,47/200)`. Every selected receiver cell
shares the same 145 nonnegative outside-mass variables.

All fixed-position maxblock caps are imposed. A full coordinate-rectangle
enumeration, deduplicating equal outside masks by their strongest RHS,
gives 7816 outside incidence rows. For each closed receiver rectangle C,
all outside bbox closures T use the exact residual bound

    nu(T) <= 4 delta_C(T)^2,
    delta_C(T)=max((b-a)/2,(d-c)/2,b-v,u-a,d-t,s-c,0),

with bbox(T)=[a,b]x[c,d] and C=[u,v]x[s,t]. Thus the entire cell,
including its boundary, is outside the strict residual halo.

For full witnesses, sort all source points by their maximum distance
from C, R_C(p). Every complete full prefix at a critical R_C<1/2
is a witness menu entry, provided it captures an internal source.
The selected entry must have mass at least `4 R_C^2 + 1/1000000`.
A positive strict margin allows a slightly enlarged **open** common
radius covering the complete closed C. This one-label whole-cell
criterion is sufficient; it is not an exhaustive characterization of
all possible unions of different full labels over C.

Binary variables select cells and full-prefix menu entries. A cell
already covered by a pure-S label only omits the full-prefix selection;
it still has its cell binary and every residual exclusion condition.
The objective is the sum of selected cell areas. The cell interiors
are disjoint, so this sum is a legitimate Lebesgue-area lower bound.

Residual rows are generated by separation. Each solve is followed by a
complete scan of all 7816 outside closures for every selected cell;
up to five most violated missing rows per cell are added. A big-M row
uses the already imposed strongest cap on the same outside mask, so
unselected cells impose no additional restriction. Once no missing
violations remain, the mass vector is rationalized, shrunk if needed,
and **all** selected-cell residual rows and all maxblock caps are checked
by Fraction arithmetic. The final actual input omits zero-mass positions.

## Actual experiments and their limits

Python 3.13.18, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1/HiGHS were actually used.
HiGHS received requested threads=2, seed=0, mip relative gap=0 and
feasibility tolerance 1e-9; BLAS/OMP were restricted to one thread.
SciPy warns that these extra HiGHS options are passed through verbatim;
the warning is preserved in the ledger. No full-machine CPU claim is made.

The first partition has 144 squares of side 4/25 in
`[-12/25,36/25]^2`. It has 272 full-label binaries plus 144 cell
binaries and 145 mass variables. Seven MIP/separation iterations took
117.63 seconds. The final exact certificate selects four cells,
area `64/625=.1024`, and zero outside mass. This coarse result is a
partition failure: only four cells are wholly covered by S alone,
although the true S-only halo area is about 1.3739936521.

The corrected partition has 225 nonuniform rectangles. In each axis,
the exact edges, in units 1/1000, are

    -480,-353,-226,-99,99,141,339,381,579,621,
    819,861,1059,1186,1313,1440.

The source-core intervals have width198, internal gaps42, and outer
strips127. All full critical prefixes are retained, so 2 by 2 and
other internal labels cover gaps as well as the 25 single-source cores.
There are 81 pure-S-covered cells with exact total area
`335241/250000=1.340964`, only about .03303 below the actual S-only halo.

This model has 340 full-label binaries, 225 cell binaries, and 145
continuous masses. Eleven MIP/separation iterations took 251.99 seconds.
The final cut matrix has 8940 rows and 710 variables. The exact positive
primal selects 89 cells, with area

    345909/250000 = 1.383636.

Its rationalization scale is 1, its smallest full-label margin is
1/1000000, and all 7816 maxcaps plus 695624 selected-cell residual
closure rows pass exactly. There are 13 positive outside atoms and
38 actual positive atoms including S. The extra R71 q happens to have

No mathematical upper bound is inferred from floating MIP optimality.
The final-cut model and any independent MATLAB solution of it do not
automatically satisfy omitted residual rows. The saved exact mass vector
was separately checked against all those rows. Its area certificate is
a positive lower bound in this specified support/partition/menu family.

The partition bias is itself measured exactly: the prior R71 33-atom
input has actual deletion area about 1.44198109, but receives only

outside mass even though its true deletion area is larger. The fixed

## Complete actual area and the useful mass contraction

The separate complete-halo engine evaluates the actual 38-atom input.
It has 816 bbox closures, 193 eligible closures, one eligible component,
maximum eligible mass1, and four maximum-mass blocks. Its total mass is
2685811/1000000 and actual span48/25, with positive nonterminal gap

S has deletion area about1.5551882181, while the other three maximum
blocks have smaller deletion costs. These are separate complete-halo
results, not inferred from the MIP cell score.

To remove accidental maximum ties, multiply **all** outside masses by
the exact factor `99999999/100000000`, leaving S fixed. Every maxcap
and residual upper constraint is preserved. Mass reduction cannot
activate a new eligible source set. A pure proper subset of S has mass
at most24/25; every other original mass1 block contains positive outside
mass and strictly decreases. Thus S becomes the unique maximum.
All 89 previous closed-cell certificates remain strict; their new
smallest full-label margin is `19907871/20000000000000`.

The complete evaluator actually confirms the contracted input has
38 positive atoms, 816 closures, 193 eligible closures, one component,
and unique maximum mask33554431=S. Its actual data are

    total mass = 268581098314189/100000000000000,
    actual span = 48/25,
    nonterminal gap = 552956311800677/660000000000000 > 0.

The exact directed complete deletion interval has endpoints displayed
approximately as1.5551882171496079. Its lower endpoint is rigorously

The contraction changes the specified-S area by only about9.25e-10,
while making it the unique available maximum-block choice.
`complete-geometry-bindings.json` retains the separate owning outputs,

It is source-bound reuse, not an independent complete-halo replay.

Maximum ties are consequently not a fundamental protection against a
strict counterexample: finite atomic area continuity and sufficiently
small outside contraction allow uniqueization, with strict connectivity
and nonterminal conditions preserved. The general lemma and independent
proof are owned by core/Astra and review. The present numerical value
remains far below3.5 and supplies no counterexample to that budget.
