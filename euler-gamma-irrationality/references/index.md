# 来源与阅读定位

各条均已核对文献身份。正文定位沿用所读版本的页码。定点精读表示阅读与本题有关的定理、证明步骤及其依赖。题录条目只承担索引作用。

1. **Euler’s constant: Euler’s work and modern developments**. Lagarias, Jeffrey (2013), Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society。定点精读 §§3.15–3.16；历史路线与联合结论。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1090/s0273-0979-2013-01423-x)

2. **Criteria for irrationality of Euler’s constant**. Sondow, Jonathan (2003), Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society。定点精读 Th. 1,4,5、Cor. 6–8；判据及量词。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0209070)

3. **A hypergeometric approach, via linear forms involving logarithms, to criteria for irrationality of Euler’s constant**. Sondow, Jonathan；Zlobin, Sergey (2009), Mathematica Slovaca。定点精读 Th. 1、Lem. 1–3、附录；同一积分的三种表示。[来源](https://doi.org/10.2478/s12175-009-0127-2)

4. **Euler’s constant, q-logarithms, and formulas of Ramanujan and Gosper**. Sondow, Jonathan；Zudilin, Wadim (2006), The Ramanujan Journal。定点精读 Th. 2–3、Lem. 7；二进制尺度。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-006-0075-1)

5. **Rational Approximation of Euler’s Constant and Recurrence Relations**. Aptekarev, A. I. (2007), Steklov Mathematical Institute。题录与总目；2007 年原始论文集，Aptekarev 主编。[来源](https://doi.org/10.4213/book232)

6. **Rational approximations for values of derivatives of the Gamma function**. Rivoal, Tanguy (2009), Transactions of the American Mathematical Society。题录与原文导论；Laguerre 行列式。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1090/s0002-9947-09-04905-8)

7. **Rational Approximations to Values of the Digamma Function and a Conjecture on Denominators**. Hessami Pilehrood, T.；Hessami Pilehrood, Kh. (2011), Mathematical Notes。定点精读 arXiv:1004.0578 定理 2 和推论 5；清分母。[来源](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mzm8780)

8. **Rational approximations for the quotient of gamma values**. Khodabakhsh Hessami Pilehrood；Tatiana Hessami Pilehrood (2010), arXiv:1010.0429。定点精读式 (1)–(3)、定理 2、推论 4；复现所用预印本。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.0429)

9. **Rational approximation of Euler’s constant using multiple orthogonal polynomials**. Van Assche, Walter；Wolfs, Thomas (2025), Combinatorics and Number Theory。定点精读 Th. 1.2、Prop. 2.5–2.8、§5；新逼近与高度。[来源](https://doi.org/10.2140/cnt.2025.14.141)

10. **On the arithmetic nature of the values of the gamma function, Euler's constant, and Gompertz's constant**. Rivoal, Tanguy (2012), Michigan Mathematical Journal。题录与 Lagarias 定理 3.16.2 对读；联合超越。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1307/mmj/1339011525)

11. **Applications of a theorem by A. B. Shidlovski**. Mahler, Kurt (1968), Proceedings of the Royal Society of London. Series A. Mathematical and Physical Sciences。题录；E 函数方法的历史来源。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1968.0111)

12. **A Note on the Irrationality of ζ(2) and ζ(3)**. Beukers, F. (1979), Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society。题录；积分证明的对照基准。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/11.3.268)

13. **On a continued fraction expansion for Euler’s constant**. Hessami Pilehrood, T.；Hessami Pilehrood, Kh. (2013), Journal of Number Theory。原文 §3 式 (23)、Th. 1；广义连分数。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.1420)

14. **RATIONAL APPROXIMATIONS TO VALUES OF BELL POLYNOMIALS AT POINTS INVOLVING EULER’S CONSTANT AND ZETA VALUES**. HESSAMI PILEHROOD, KH.；HESSAMI PILEHROOD, T. (2012), Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society。题录及 mixed §5 引用；参数 a 的逼近族。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1017/s1446788712000134)

15. **Multiple orthogonal polynomials associated with the exponential integral**. Van Assche, Walter；Wolfs, Thomas (2023), Studies in Applied Mathematics。摘要；混合多重正交结构的前置工作。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.04858)

16. **Euler–Lehmer constants and a conjecture of Erdös**. Murty, M. Ram；Saradha, N. (2010), Journal of Number Theory。题录；广义常数族的结论范围。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jnt.2010.07.004)

17. **Double integrals for Euler’s constant and ln(4/π) and an analog of Hadjicostas’s formula**. Sondow, Jonathan (2005), American Mathematical Monthly。题录；双积分归一化的参考。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0211148)

18. **An Asymptotic Expansion for the Error Term in the Brent-McMillan Algorithm for Euler’s Constant**. Paris, R. B. (2019), Journal of Mathematics Research。题录；高精度 γ 的另一种余项分析。[来源](https://doi.org/10.5539/jmr.v11n3p60)

19. **Properties and Applications of the Reciprocal Logarithm Numbers**. Kowalenko, Victor (2010), Acta Applicandae Mathematicae。原主张经 Coffey–Sondow §2 对读；有理级数论证。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10440-008-9325-0)

20. **Rebuttal of Kowalenko’s Paper As Concerns the Irrationality of Euler’s Constant γ**. Coffey, Mark W.；Sondow, Jonathan (2012), Acta Applicandae Mathematicae。正文 §§2–4；已发表反驳与审稿记录。[来源](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10440-012-9697-z)

21. **Irrationalité de ζ2 et ζ3**. Apéry, Roger (1979), Astérisque。题录与原文；递推及整数化范例。[来源](https://www.numdam.org/item/AST_1979__61__11_0/)

22. **On linear forms containing the Euler constant**. Aptekarev, A. I. (2009), arXiv:0902.1768v2。摘要及 v2 说明；γ、δ 的析取。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.1768)

23. **Rational approximation to Euler’s constant at a geometric rate of convergence**. Adell, José A.；Lekuona, Alberto (2020), Mathematics of Computation。原文导论；按迭代指标几何收敛。[来源](https://zaguan.unizar.es/record/130459/files/texto_completo.pdf)

24. **An Infinite Family of Apéry-like Recurrences for Euler’s Constant**. Kalisch, Rotem；Monderer, Tali；Leibtag, Elyasheev；Weinbaum, Shachar；Shalyt, Michael；Kaminer, Ido (2026), arXiv:2610.13919v1。摘要与正文目录；Meijer G 函数递推，预印本。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13919)

25. **Transcendence Results for $\Gamma^{(n)}(1)$ and Related Sequences of Generalized Constants**. Powers, Michael R. (2026), arXiv:2511.01849v7。v7 摘要；成对结论与指标选择。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01849)

26. **On irrationality of Euler’s constant and related asymptotic formulas**. Suman, Shekhar (2023), arXiv:2312.00295v1。核查正文第 9 页 (58)–(60)；见报告质疑。[来源](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00295)

27. **On the Irrationality of Notable Constants**. Aquilante, Francesco (2026), viXra:2602.0073v2。v1 §4 与 v2 版本说明；修订放弃 γ 主张。[来源](https://vixra.org/abs/2602.0073)
