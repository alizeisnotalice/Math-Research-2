# Independent audit of the upper Gevrey-2 zero-type claim

The proof in round036-upper-gevrey-zero-type.md is valid. Under the smooth quartic-germ and exact-graph assumptions, it establishes limsup (|[u^k]w|/k!)^(1/k)=0. This does not imply a smaller Gevrey order on its own.

I checked the full triangular identity by setting n=k+4 and j=i+1 in the existing diagonal coefficient formula. The factor after division by k! is exactly R_{k,i}/(2^i(i+1)!), with the i=0 coefficient h_k.

For a composition into 2i+1 labeled slots, fix one maximum once (lowest index resolves ties). A normalized analytic coefficient of g⁻¹ has a bound M q^j for each prescribed q>0. A V slot instead has the record bound H_k q^j when j≤k. Thus either kind of maximum obeys the stated estimate; every remaining slot, including other equal or comparably large V slots, is bounded by M L^j. Start with the chosen largest part and adjoin the others. Each multinomial factor is at least 2^j because the accumulated order N≥j implies (N+l)/l≥2 for 1≤l≤j. This proves the common geometric majorant α^(sum of nonmaximal parts), α=L/(2q)<1. The majorant remains valid for any number of high V slots. For fixed i, summing over possible maximal positions merely overcounts the bound. Extending the array by zero when the other components are incompatible with the maximum makes dominated convergence on the fixed countable lattice literal. As n→∞ at fixed other indices and fixed i, any nonzero remaining index j supplies an additional binomial factor tending to infinity, so those terms vanish after the record normalization.

For n>0 each one-part V contribution is exactly c^i h_n and there are i+1 of them. The one-part g⁻¹ contribution is i (g⁻¹(0))^(i−1)V(0)^(i+1) [u^n]g⁻¹/n!; analyticity makes it negligible on the normalized scale. For n=0 the singleton classification is inapplicable but the uniform majorant includes the all-zero composition. In the sum over i this only matters for a growing i and is absorbed by its factorial tail. For i=0 the convolution is exactly h_n. No boundary issue arises when k is odd.

Since R_{k,i}→1 at fixed i and is uniformly ≤256^i, multiplying by the triangular weights and summing is justified by a summable bound C^(i+1)/(q^(2i)(i+1)!). The resulting formal symbol exp(cz²/2) is invertible with coefficients (−c/2)^i/i!. In the inverse identity at a record, each fixed lag is o(1) using H_{k−2i}≤H_k; the entire series is dominated by a fixed exponential generating sum. This contradicts the normalized record value one. Finally multiplication by the analytic g⁻¹ preserves zero factorial type by a direct normalized convolution (use q/2 on both factors).

Independent Fraction checks exhaust 57,040 small labeled compositions, including maxima in g⁻¹ or V, ties and unique maxima. They verify all 48 finite singleton identities and 31 orders of the formal inverse. The file r036_upper_type_independent_check.json contains the actual outputs.
