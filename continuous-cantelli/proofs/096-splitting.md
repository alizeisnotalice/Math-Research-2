# Early reflection symmetry and the scale of asymmetric splitting at unequal tails

**The R084 early slab is necessarily symmetric.** Write J_t={f<t}, Q_t for the Brownian surviving law there, and V(t,y)=∫₀ᵗQ_s(y)ds. In distributions
(∂_t−½∂²_y)V=δ₀−g1_{J_t}.
This follows by integrating the heat equation for the graph sources, or directly from the heat primitive. By nonnegative survival and increasing J_t, V≥0 and V(t,·)=0 almost everywhere off J_t. Let V* and J* denote the spatial reflections and W=V−V*. The point source cancels. On each of the two parts of the symmetric difference of J_t and J_t*, the product W(1_{J_t}−1_{J_t*}) is nonnegative, since the potential from the excluded side is zero. The L² energy identity from zero initial data gives
∥W(t)∥²₂/2+½∫₀ᵗ∥∂_y W∥²₂ds=−∫₀ᵗ∫ Wg(1_{J_s}−1_{J_s*})dy ds≤0.
It applies directly to the mild heat solution with bounded L² forcing, or by spatial mollification and passage to the limit. Thus W≡0, then the equation forces the interval indicators to coincide for almost all times. Monotonicity and continuity of the early endpoints extend this to every early time. Hence A(t)=B(t), the two terminal radii agree (write R=A=B), and f is even on the entire open early core (−R,R). This uses positivity from R084 and imposes no analytic assumption.

**Nearby sign requirement.** Suppose lim_{r→∞} f(−r)=1+T=L and liminf_{r→∞} f(r)>L. For all sufficiently small η>0 the order f(−r)≤f(r) throughout {r≥0:min(f(−r),f(r))≤T+η} is impossible. To see it take T<t<T+η and use the primitive of Q from T to t. By early symmetry the time-T evolution baseline is even. The discrepancy from sources activated after T has, on y>0, the integrated reflected heat kernel K_{t−s}(y−r)−K_{t−s}(y+r), where K_h=∫₀ʰ p_v dv, K_h=0 for h≤0. This difference is nonnegative and nondecreasing in h. Under the proposed order the primitive on the left therefore lies below its reflected value on the right (the early symmetric source terms cancel). For all s up to t the right exterior above a fixed large R' has Q_s=0 by the R083 one-sided resolvent: the right tail has infimum >1+t far enough, and all activations up to t on that side lie in a compact interval. Hence this primitive is nonpositive on its left exterior and zero on its right exterior. Its negative real Laplace transform is bounded above by C exp(R'λ). But for all s in a fixed closed interval inside (T,t], the positive future transform of Q_s receives ≥c exp(c'λ²) from the window |x+λ|≤1 on the critical left tail. Add in s to obtain a contradiction.

**Outer asymmetry matched to the lower far tail.** Suppose additionally that f(±r)>T for r>R (no simultaneous outside occurrence of level T), and that
f(−r)=L−D/r+o(1/r)  as r→∞,  D<2R.
On a small collar write V(u)=f(R+u)−T and Δ(u)=f(−R−u)−f(R+u), 0<u<ε. Suppose V(u)∼c u^p with c,p>0, and Δ>0. If
−log Δ(u)∼K/u^γ  for K,γ>0,
then necessarily
γ=p,   K=(R−D/2)²/(2c).                                               (1)
A nonzero split of fixed positive power order Δ(u)≍u^m is impossible in this setting. A split of the opposite fixed sign cannot occur either.

Indeed subtract the original exact identities at λ and −λ, multiply by exp(Tλ²/2), and use early symmetry. The positive collar term is
J(λ)=∫₀^ε 2sinh(λ(R+u)) g(R+u) exp(−λ² V(u)/2) [1−exp(−λ² Δ(u)/2)]du.
All compact parts outside the collar and a large fixed radius have an exponential-in-λ² gap by f>T. On the far region r≥R₀ the high right tail contributes O(exp(−c'λ²)) by completing the square, and the disfavored exp(−λr) term on the lower tail does too. Thus J(λ) agrees to such exponential accuracy with
I(λ)=∫_{R₀}^∞ p₁(r−λ) exp[λ²(L−f(−r))/2]dr.
Its linear logarithmic rate is D/2: restrict to |r−λ|≤1 for a lower bound; on |r−λ|≤aλ the exponent divided by λ lies within O(a)+o(1) of D/2. Outside that region, enlarge R₀ if necessary so that |L−f|<a²/4, and use Gaussian tail decay. The fixed bounded interval removed in this enlargement has f>T by hypothesis and contributes exponentially little. Finally send a↓0. The opposite fixed sign at the collar would make both it and the negative low far contribution have the same sign, contradicting exactness.

For γ=p set q=λ²/2. At small u, use V≥(c−a)u^p, Δ≤exp(−(K−a)/u^p), and 1−exp(−qΔ)≤qΔ. The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives qu^p(c−a)+(K−a)/u^p≥√(2(c−a)(K−a))λ. Shrinking the collar and discarding its compact outer portion gives limsup λ^{−1}log J≤R−√(2cK). For the reverse bound use u near [K/(qc)]^{1/(2p)} within a fixed relative window, of polynomial length. There qΔ→0 and 1−exp(−qΔ)≥qΔ/2; taking the relative width and a to zero gives equality in the rate. If γ>p the minimized damping q(c/2)u^p+(K/2)u^{−γ} is a positive multiple of λ^{2γ/(p+γ)}, which exceeds linear order. If γ<p its value is o(λ) on a fixed relative window about u=λ^{−2/(p+γ)}, and λ^{-1}log J→R by this lower bound and the trivial upper bound on successively smaller collars. For Δ≍u^m one can instead select u near λ^{-2/p}; all non-spatial factors on that window have polynomial lower bounds, again giving rate R. Equating to D/2 proves (1). The power and coefficient in (1) are necessary conditions on an actual unequal-tail candidate, not an existence result.
