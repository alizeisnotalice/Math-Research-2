# An exact relaxed law with local determinism on a dense open set

Fix ε=1/8, s=1−ε, J as in round 128, K any compact subset of J of positive measure. Let η=(1−ε/2)1_Kγ and define subprobabilities μ=γ−P_εη and λ=γ−η of equal mass. Round 128 gives P_εη≤0.08 ε γ, so μ≥η and λ≥εγ/2. Start Brownian motion with the normalized μ. A randomized embedding of the normalized λ stops the submass μ−η immediately and lets η evolve for time ε. Consequently μ precedes λ in convex order.

Use the regular Root embedding from μ to λ with barrier r. Normalize both measures by their common mass, and translate by their common mean if centered laws are used. The comparison above is a stopping time with a uniform randomizer in the initial filtration independent of the Brownian increments. Root optimality for the residual expectation E(τ−ε)_+ gives τ≤ε almost surely. The atom-free target density is bounded below locally by a positive constant, so the regular barrier is continuous and finite at every x. Hence τ=r(B_τ) by continuity of the path and barrier, including τ=0. Full support of λ gives r≤ε everywhere. [Cox, Obłój and Touzi, Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.03169) · [Bayraktar and Bernhardt, Theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.14695).

Assign η the mark s and assign λ the mark 1+r(x). The spatial marginal is γ. By the bounded-time exponential Brownian martingale the late contribution to the transform is exp(−z²/2)∫exp(zx)μ(dx). Since μ=γ−P_εη, adding the early contribution cancels the removed term exactly. At s the heat remainder is p_s dx−η, strictly negative on K since p_s/g<1−ε there. A smooth nonnegative spatial test also witnesses negativity by regularity and approximation, with persistence for a short time after s. On K exactly two marks have positive conditional mass; everywhere else the single mark is continuous. Taking K nowhere dense and of arbitrarily small positive mass gives the claimed localization.

The accompanying explicit set starts from the interval of radius 3/20000 centered at 2+1/5000. At stage n≥1 remove from every surviving interval its central relative fraction 2^(−n)/256. The resulting compact has positive length, no interior, and the sum of all removed lengths is at most 1/256 of the original interval length. At t=7/8+10^(−12) the certified smooth test has pairing below −311/2400000000. The supplementary proof and rational script detail the bounds on the heat kernel and on the test's second derivative. Only the supplementary finite-stage illustrations use truncation of the Cantor iteration; the infinite-stage measure estimate is analytic.
