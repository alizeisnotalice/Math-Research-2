# A single regular crossing, the entire spatial heat solution, and the opposite-end tail budget

Adopt the assumptions and endpoint notation in r088-critical-tail-budget.md. Take the lower far wing on the left, so f(−r)→L=1+T. Suppose only its finite crossing −A has an inward real analytic monotone branch with outward analytic continuation f_syn. The nonzero inward slope follows from the R084 boundary-speed estimate. Suppose the actual f is ≤f_syn on a small outward collar, for example if the actual graph is analytic at −A. No regularity is imposed at the opposite crossing B. There exist constants K≥0 and η>0 with the following budget. Let ℓ(s) be the continued inverse of f_syn, w(s)=−g(ℓ(s))ℓ′(s)>0, and
J(λ)=∫_T^{T+η} exp[−λℓ(s)−(s−T)λ²/2] w(s)ds.
Then
H(λ)−J(λ) = e^{−Bλ} [K/λ²+O(λ^{−3})],     λ→∞.                      (1)
In particular the entire remote left-wing contribution to H is bounded by the right-hand side in (1), since the disjoint actual finite left collar contributes at least J. It follows that if f(−r)=L+D/r+o(1/r) then D≥2B. This strengthens the lower bound −2A available for D from the unmarked compact support. At D=2B, an expansion f(−r)=L+2B/r+[β log r+C+o(1)]/r² requires β≥4, and β=4 requires exp[(B²−C)/2]≤K. A one-sided upper bound of the indicated forms suffices to enforce these restrictions. If in addition the right outward slope has finite positive value c_o, the right collar uses another 2g(B)/c_o of the normalized budget, so exp[(B²−C)/2]+2g(B)/c_o≤K at β=4.

**Construction.** On a fixed complex neighborhood of T define analytic coefficients
c₀=0, c₁=2w, c_{j+2}=2(c'_j−ℓ′c_{j+1}).
The series U(t,y)=∑_{j≥0} c_j(t)(y−ℓ(t))^j/j! is holomorphic for t in a fixed smaller disk and for **every** complex y. Indeed choose concentric time disks of radii 2r and r. To bound c_N take the disks of radii 2r−jr/N in the induction j≤N. With B_j the sup of c_j on the jth disk, Cauchy's estimate gives B_{j+2}≤2N B_j/r+2∥ℓ′∥ B_{j+1}. Hence B_N≤C^N N^{N/2} for a fixed C, and division by N! gives entire spatial convergence, uniform on compact subsets. Direct differentiation gives U_t=U_{yy}/2, U(t,ℓ(t))=0 and U_y(t,ℓ(t))=2w(t).

At time T the true recent graph source near −A is exactly w(s)δ_{ℓ(s)} ds from times s<T. In that spatial region the distribution V(t,y)=1_{y>ℓ(t)}U(t,y) has the very same heat source with sign −w(s)δ_{ℓ(s)}, by differentiation across its zero boundary and the specified derivative jump. Thus q_T−V(T,·) is spatially real analytic across −A. For a self-contained localization take a fixed smooth spatial cutoff equal to one near −A and supported away from the right edge, and replace V by the cut-off V for t∈[T−η₀,T] with η₀ small. Its additional smooth heat forcing is spatially separated from −A, as are all remaining right sources. Applying the heat semigroup from time T−η₀ to the difference gives a positive-age convolution plus integrals over sources at distance ≥c. All are holomorphic in y on a common small complex disk about −A by the off-diagonal bound C s^{−1/2}exp(−c′/s), s↓0. Duhamel's formula here follows directly by differentiating the bounded, cut-off V against a backward heat kernel; the other true sources are finite positive measures. Outside the left edge both q_T and V(T,·) vanish, and hence their difference vanishes in an interval inside it. At every interior point of (−A,B) the true q_T is real analytic by the identical off-diagonal argument: take an earlier time sufficiently close to T that its two later boundary sources are separated from that point. It follows by spatial uniqueness and the whole-line analyticity of U(T,·) that
q_T(y)=U(T,y) on the entire interval (−A,B).
Static right-barrier domination from R084 gives q_T(B−u)≤Cu, so U(T,B)=0 and U_y(T,B)≤0.

**Right-truncated identity.** For t near or above T set M(t)=∫_{ℓ(t)}^B e^{−λy}U(t,y)dy. Its initial value is H(λ). Twice spatial integration by parts and the moving zero boundary give
M′(t)=λ²M(t)/2−w(t)e^{−λℓ(t)}+½e^{−λB}[U_y(t,B)+λU(t,B)].
Integrate from T to T+η with factor exp[−(t−T)λ²/2]. The final M term is O(exp(−ηλ²/2+Cλ)), since spatial integration is over a fixed compact range and U bounded there. Also U(T+s,B)=O(s), U_y(T+s,B)=U_y(T,B)+O(s). Therefore (1) follows with K=−U_y(T,B). The right-side budget for the far tail follows by completing the Gaussian square with x=−λ+z; for D=2B the finite-z limiting factor after multiplication by exp(Bλ)λ^{β/2} is exp(−Bz−C/2), whose Gaussian integral is exp[(B²−C)/2]. The right collar contributes the stated amount by x=B+z/λ² and positivity. These two disjoint pieces remain in H−J under the one-sided comparison hypothesis.

**Finite jets on one wing.** There is also a smooth single-crossing conclusion which requires no analyticity. If the left graph is C∞ through −A with nonzero slope, then for every integer k,
H(λ)−J(λ)=o(e^{Aλ} λ^{−k}),
where J is the actual future left collar defined through its smooth inverse. Consequently f(−r)=L−2A/r+[β log r+C+o(1)]/r² is impossible for every finite β,C, with no assumption at B. To prove this, form only the finite polynomial U_M in the above recurrence. Its residual U_{M,t}−U_{M,yy}/2 is O((y−ℓ(t))^{M−1}) at that left edge. After multiplying by the half-space indicator the residual has uniformly bounded continuous spatial derivatives through M−2 there. The cutoff and Duhamel argument now imply q_T−V_M(T,·) is C^{M−2} across −A (spatial derivatives inside the local forcing may be passed to that forcing); it vanishes to that order because it vanishes on the left. Watson's elementary integral ∫₀∞e^{−λu}u^jdu=j!/λ^{j+1} gives H(λ)e^{−Aλ} the full formal expansion ∑ c_j(T)λ^{−j−1}. For the collar define V_λ(t)=e^{λℓ(t)}∫_t^{T+η} exp[−λℓ(s)−(s−t)λ²/2]w(s)ds. It satisfies V_λ′=(λℓ′+λ²/2)V_λ−w. Substitution of the same finite expansion cancels each order by the c_j recurrence. For a truncation j≤M the remaining forcing is O(λ^{−M+1}) or smaller; backward integration over an interval with positive coefficient ≥λ²/4 makes the residual O(λ^{−M−1}), with an exponentially small terminal term. Taking M arbitrarily large proves matching to every order and therefore the stated exclusion by positivity of the remote contribution.
