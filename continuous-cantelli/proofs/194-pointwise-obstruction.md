# A limitation of pointwise fractional heat tests

Fix 0<d<1 and 0<κ<1. There exists a compact strongly separated homogeneous Cantor set E of dimension d, with its d-regular self-similar probability μ, such that for arbitrarily small b>0 the fractional heat difference of K_b=P_bμ is at most −c b^(−(1−d)/2−κ) at the left endpoint of E. The assertion still holds at points strictly outside E whose distance is b^(1/p) for any given 0<p<2. Thus a fixed higher-order pointwise positivity step cannot follow from d-regularity alone, even for a vanishing ratio dist/√b.

Put F(y)=∫₀^∞(p₁(y)−p_{1+t}(y))t^(−1−κ)dt. It is bounded and continuous by the uniform heat derivative bound on ages 1 to 2 and direct integration at t≥1. It satisfies F(0)>0 and F(y)<0 for all sufficiently large |y|. To see the latter, write the integral for 0<t<1 plus the tail. For large |y| the short part is nonpositive, since p_a(y) increases in a for 1≤a≤2. On the tail the full positive term is p₁(y)/κ, whereas ∫_{y²}^{2y²}p_{1+t}(y)t^(−1−κ)dt≥c|y|^(−1−2κ). The Gaussian term is smaller for large |y|. Choose D and a small closed interval around D on which F≤−η<0 and F<0 farther to the right.

Use similarities of common ratio ρ=M^(−1/d) mapping the full hull [0,1] to M disjoint subintervals. Put one interval at 0 and the other M−1 together near 1, separated by gaps at least ρ, the last interval ending at 1. This is possible because Mρ→0 as M→∞. The natural probability is d-regular: a ball of radius between ρ^(n+1) and ρ^n contains a full generation-(n+1) cylinder at any E center and intersects at most a bounded number of generation-n cylinders since their hulls are disjoint equal intervals. All constants may depend on M. Pick M so large that D times the entire right cluster lies in the negative interval, and ||F||∞/M−η(1−1/M)<−η/2 (shrinking η if needed).

At b_n=(ρ^n/D)², the world coordinates in the n-fold leftmost prefix have normalized spatial distances |x|/√b_n distributed as D times the original μ. This prefix has mass M^(−n), whose test integral is at most −ηM^(−n)/2. All other points of E lie at normalized distance at least D(1−O(Mρ))/ρ, hence contribute nonpositively. Scaling the heat operator gives the prefactor b_n^(−1/2−κ). Since M^(−n)=ρ^(nd)=D^d b_n^(d/2), the asserted negative rate follows. At x_n=−b_n^(1/p), normalized distances increase by b_n^(1/p−1/2)→0. Uniform continuity on the compact relevant interval for the two first-level clusters preserves their strict negative mean, and all remaining distances remain in the negative tail. This proves the same bound at x_n with the variance profile b=dist(x,E)^p.

An illustration using Python mpmath 1.3.0, 30 decimal working digits, and quadrature on [0,1,10,100,∞] gives F(0)=1.35195648, F(7.98)=−0.03761963, F(8)=−0.03747253, F(8.02)=−0.03732641 for κ=1/4. The tail comparison and construction above establish the signs without numerical assumptions. The example concerns the pointwise fractional test and does not assert existence of an exact graph.
