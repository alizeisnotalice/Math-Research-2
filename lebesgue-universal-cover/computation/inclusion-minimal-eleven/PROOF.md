# A new inclusion-minimal eleven-point obstruction and a tube joining two low six-body placements

All statements concern compact convex planar hosts. Each input may move by an independent Euclidean isometry, including reflection. The normalized original five inputs are the radius-half disk and the regular Reuleaux bodies of orders 3, 5, 7 and 9. Disk center and triangle angle fix the coordinate frame. The original sixth input is the repository's P30. The new rational set Q has the coordinates in `new-points.json`, with denominator one billion. Its squared diameter is $19999999890783901/20000000000000000$. It is a separate input from P30 and from both previous eleven-point sets.

## The integer translation certificate

Let H and P be counterclockwise convex polygons and let $n_e=(dy,-dx)$ for each directed edge. Write $h_X(n)=\max_{x\in X}n\cdot x$. Then
\[
  2\bigl(|\operatorname{conv}(H\cup(P+t))|-|H|\bigr)
  =\sum_{e\in E(H)\sqcup E(P)}[h_P(n_e)+n_e\cdot t-h_H(n_e)]_+.
\]
For a derivation, lift the polygons to heights zero and one and project their convex hull along $(-t,1)$. On the lower visible surface the bottom base projects to H. A lateral facet with horizontal unit outer normal direction $u$ has end faces with lengths $L_H,L_P$ (a vertex has length zero). Its projected signed area is ((L_H+L_P)(h_P$u$+u\cdot t-h_H$u$)/2). The lower visible facets contribute the positive parts and tile the rest of the projected polygon up to edges. Parallel edges in the two polygons contribute their separate lengths.

For each proposed translation, choose an arbitrary subset of terms and replace their positive parts by the corresponding affine functions, discarding other terms. This is an affine lower bound everywhere. A target area imposes a half-plane on translations. `hinge.cpp` uses floating-point centroids only to choose subsets. A reported exclusion requires a constant strict contradiction or two or three exact positive integer weights whose weighted normals cancel and whose weighted allowed bounds sum to a negative number. A base radius of at least 0.48 is checked by integer arithmetic, and $0\in P$ is also checked. The initial translation square of half-width 3 therefore contains every placement of target area at most one, since the hull of the base disk and (t) contains a triangle of area $0.48|t|$.

All input vertices are bounded by $S=10^7$ in either coordinate. For a convex polygon the total variation of either coordinate around its boundary is at most $4S$. Thus both polygons together give each accumulated cut coefficient absolute value at most $8S$. Its constant has magnitude below $35S^2$, including the area threshold. Two-by-two normal determinants fit in signed 64 bits, and all cross-multiplied feasibility comparisons and the weighted contradictions fit in signed 128 bits (each term is below $10^{33}$). Tests using 160-bit Arb and integer cross products construct the inner polygons on the same grid.

For a closed angle interval of width $2\pi/N$, the centered support of Q lies between 0.47 and 0.51 and has angular slope of magnitude below 0.2. The latter follows from $h^2+(h')^2\le 0.51^2$ on each exposed-vertex interval and $0.51^2-0.47^2<0.2^2$. Contract the midpoint polygon by the factor $1-(0.2\pi/N+2/S)/0.47$ and round coordinates to grid $S$. Its support is below every rotated support in the closed interval; the additional $2/S$ covers vertex rounding. The same proof handles the reflected polygon.

For the tube and small central boxes, `template.py` contracts the midpoint regular body about its center by the independent translation/rotation support displacement divided by its inradius. It then samples circular arcs and uses the repository's verified rounding. `hinge_bank.py` checks that the resulting host polygon contains a 0.49-disk and performs one further contraction before the final rounding. The auxiliary pair-of-chords proof in `verify.py` also reaches 0.83755 at H*, independently of the hinge identity.

For the full stated 181/114819 subbox, `corner_core.py` uses coordinatewise translation extrema selected by the signs of the test normal. For the three freely rotating regular bodies enumerate the eight combinations of endpoint angles. The centered support as a function of angle is continuously differentiable. On an exposed-vertex interval $h''=-h$, and on an exposed unit circular arc $h''=1-h$. Hence an intermediate support exceeds the smaller endpoint support minus the angular width squared divided by eight. For each fixed normal, the individual bodies' angles can attain their smaller endpoint independently. The maximum of these three individual lower supports is therefore the minimum of the eight endpoint-host supports at that normal, including the fixed-angle triangle and disk via the same maximum. For each of four sign quadrants enforce the eight corner-hull half-plane systems for normals in that quadrant, including the cardinal boundaries and the interpolation and rounding losses. Within each vertex normal cone support interpolates linearly in the normal. The endpoint losses thus suffice also between neighboring facet normals by the triangle inequality. SciPy proposes vertices of the simultaneous intersection. Every accepted grid vertex satisfies every exact integer half-plane. The terminal addresses have the full prefix property and use the deterministic midpoint split in `replay_hinges.py`.

For the independent chord proofs, a forward chord from integer vertex i to j has outer normal $n=(y_j-y_i,x_i-x_j)$. A proposed exterior point creates a triangle whose signed area minus the host's intervening cap is a lower bound for exterior area. For two chords, the two gains may be summed if their exterior half-planes are disjoint within the disk of radius 0.706. For a target at most 0.83755, an exterior point of radius 0.706 would already give too great an area with the base disk of radius 0.5, as checked using the exact disk-and-point hull formula. The verifier checks disjointness using the strict separation of the sum of the two unit normals, balances aggregate integer normals, subtracts the angular support errors for each term, and treats both orientations over the entire angle circle.

## Reproduction

Use the `lebesgue-universal-cover/computation` directory of the public Math-Research-2 repository, including the R158 stationary enclosure and the R155/R175 input poses. The workspace checkout at verification was a7b5edbc67492f76fb0f9ff961ccbfa100a04aef. Dependencies are Python, python-flint, NumPy/SciPy, and a C++17 compiler with signed 128-bit integers. Supply a scratch directory on the desired data disk. Run from this package's directory.

```sh
c++ -std=c++17 -O3 hinge.cpp -o "$SCRATCH/hinge"
python -B replay_hinges.py "$COMPUTATION" "$SCRATCH" --kernel "$SCRATCH/hinge" --output "$SCRATCH/hinge-audit.json"
python -B verify.py "$COMPUTATION" . --output "$SCRATCH/chord-audit.json"
python -B verify_deletions.py "$COMPUTATION" "$SCRATCH/deletions.json"
python -B check6.py "$COMPUTATION" bundle-pose.json "$SCRATCH/bundle.json" --k 8192
python -B check6.py "$COMPUTATION" other-pose.json "$SCRATCH/other.json" --k 8192
python -B check6.py "$COMPUTATION" new-six-pose.json "$SCRATCH/new-six.json" --k 8192 --cloud new-points.json
python -B check6.py "$COMPUTATION" seven-pose.json "$SCRATCH/seven.json" --k 8192
```

The exploratory multistart scanned 8792 canonical original-leaf centers, then optimized 256 separated candidates, followed by a nonsmooth refinement of 27 deduplicated starts. The two supplied original-six upper bounds are certified separately. This search produced no original-six curved placement with area below 0.83750.
