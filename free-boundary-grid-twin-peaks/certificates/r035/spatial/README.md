# r035 exact n=7 spatial CRT certificate

This finite certificate reconstructs from 40 four-prime residue files the five
`h` bins, squared-Euclidean weighted `E` bins, and three Manhattan-far `h`
vectors at all ten D4 hole orbits of the 7-by-7 grid. It makes no asymptotic,
tail, or peak-distance conclusion.

The portable auditor verifies Pratt certificates, direct and Garner CRT,
modulus bounds, raw metadata, D4 multiplicity 49, all five global h bins
against public r033 exact data, and source-specified far-support nesting. It
records per-orbit R45 residuals but never assumes they vanish.

`far1`, `far2`, `far3` mean strictly `2d_1>n`, `2d_1>2n`, `2d_1>3n`.
`E` weights the low-root-side directed-edge contribution by squared Euclidean
distance, not a rounded floating-point Q value.
