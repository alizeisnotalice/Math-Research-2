# r024 weighted first-merge geometry

`top-row-arm-mass.md` gives an exact, no-multiplicity top-row arm family with
\(F_n/A_2=((2^{n-1}-2)/(4R_n))p_n\), reducing its normalization to a
boundary-prefix one-peak surface probability \(p_n\). The retained n=3,…,5
integer DP verifies this identity at finite size only.

`balanced-far-first-merge.md` gives actual positive-weight fibres having
bounded balance \(m<2\) but macroscopic conditional root distance. Hence a
bound on the first-merge imbalance alone cannot imply \(Q_n\to0\). Neither
document proves a nonvanishing normalized grid mass.

`top-row-branch-swap.md` gives four disjoint reversible image families, hence
the all-\(n\) strict bound \(4F_n\le A_2(n)\). The retained full 3×3
enumeration `audit_top_row_branch_swaps_n3.py`/JSON verifies its four images;
the same audit records a 4×4 two-step attempted iteration with three peaks.
Thus the factor four is not an iterated \(n\)-scale conclusion.
