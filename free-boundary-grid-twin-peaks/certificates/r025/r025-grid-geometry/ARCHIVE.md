# Top-row one-peak surface quantities

This package gives exact finite values of the top-row single-peak probability
through n=5 and the basic completion sandwich between the first row and the
first two rows.  The finite values are compatible with a factorial-decay
guess but prove no uniform inequality.  The included comparison only shows
that a simple fixed-boundary-strip injection cannot provide a constant-base
surface-cost bound.  It does not compare with all one-peak words and does
not settle the top-row two-peak arm mass.
