# A022 r030 certificates

- `r030-short-tail-crt`: finite n=7 center h1--h3 recovery only.
- `r030-integrated-tail`: QE8 sufficient-candidate analysis, finite checks,
  and a center-hole counterexample to its invalid per-hole strengthening.
- `r030-corner-far` and `r030-corner-far-parent`: finite construction audit
  and its general lower-bound proof; the bound tends to zero.
- `r030-gap-geometry`: exact finite n=5 joint L--geometry table and r29
  cross-check, not an asymptotic result.
- `r030-spatial-geometry`: local path-kernel identities and stated endpoint/
  midpoint obstructions, not a global weighted-geometry bound.

GPU jobs and later noncentral Mac short-tail jobs are outside this cutoff.
