  tail contraction can hold uniformly in all sufficiently large square grids;
  it does not refute `A2 <= 5B` or settle the original problem.
- `r029-corner-deletion`: the analytic corner-buffer deletion lemma and
  independent finite n=3--5 audits.
- `r029-tail-full-n5`: exact complete punctured first-neighbour tail for n=5
  across all six D4 hole classes; its 4/5 contraction is finite only.
- `r029-short-tail-ie`: exact formulas for h1--h3 and n=3,4 all-hole checks;
  no control of the remaining tail is claimed.
- `r029-short-tail-mod`: corrected modular implementation evidence only;
  the earlier directed-edge-union candidate outputs are excluded as invalid.
- `r029-punctured-orbits-n7`: full finite n=7 D4 selected-hole table, with
  no historical binary artifact included.
- `r029-merge-rb-n24`: finite raw/trace integrity and arithmetic replay only;
  no SMC accuracy, mixing, conditional-law or asymptotic conclusion.
